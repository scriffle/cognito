🧬 Welcome to the Genetics Simulator

This tool explores how traits are inherited across generations — a topic that connects deeply to the knowledge systems of the Kulin Nation peoples, the Traditional Owners of the land where this curriculum is taught.

For tens of thousands of years, Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples have observed, understood, and cared for the biodiversity of Country. Knowledge of animal behaviour, plant growth, and seasonal patterns has been passed through generations — an inheritance of wisdom, much like the inheritance of genes we explore here.

As you learn about genetic diversity, consider how this knowledge connects to the custodianship of Country: healthy populations need genetic diversity, and caring for habitat is caring for the future of all species.
