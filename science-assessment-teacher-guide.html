Science Check-in: Teacher Guide

A quick how-to for running the science check-in with your class.

› What it is and what it assesses

A short adaptive science diagnostic for Years 7 to 10, assessed against the Victorian Curriculum 2.0 (Science). It checks what a student already knows across the science topics. It is not pass or fail. It shows each student's strengths and gaps so you can plan support or extension.

The report shows, per Victorian Curriculum 2.0 strand and per level (Levels 7-8 and 9-10), how far the student got across the thinking levels: Remember, Apply, Analyse, and Evaluate.

› How it works

Every student answers the same opening questions, then continues to more demanding cognitive levels on the Bloom's scale only while they keep getting them right. A student who is struggling finishes sooner. A strong student goes further.

Question types: true/false, multiple choice, and cloze (fill in the blank).

› Step by step
  1. Send the student the link by email, or share it as a OneDrive link.
  2. The student enters their name and student number (both required), chooses a scope, and presses Begin.
    Tip: the student number lets you track the same student over time (longitudinal tracking), so keep it consistent.
  3. The student works through the questions. No results are shown to them.
  4. At the end, enter the passcode to save the responses. The PDF saves automatically.
  5. Help the student to find the saved PDF in their Downloads folder (named studentnumber-name-science-responses.pdf).
  6. Help the student to press Open email to teacher, type your email address, attach that PDF, and send.
› Security and privacy
  • Nothing is sent or stored automatically. There is no server and no cloud. The results exist only as the PDF on the device until a person emails it.
  • The student never sees their results. They only see a saved message.
  • The PDF is password-protected. It opens only with the passcode.
  • The email contains no scores. All the detail is inside the locked PDF.
  • Only staff with the passcode can open a student's results.

› What to tell the students
  • "This is a science check-in, not a test you can fail."
  • "It helps me see what you already know and what to help you with next."
  • "The questions get harder as you go. Do your best, take your time, and answer what you can. It is fine to guess or skip."
  • "You will not see a score. Your answers come straight to me."
