# VC2HE8K09 — Economics and Business (Short)

## Snapshot
- **Descriptor:** The Australian taxation system and how taxation revenue is used.
- **Year / Area:** Year 8, Economics and Business.
- **Big ideas:** Tax funds shared services. Income tax is progressive. Trust matters for compliance.

## What you are learning
- You are learning about the **Australian taxation system** and **how taxation revenue is used**.
- You should be able to read tax as the price of shared services.

## Core content

Tax funds shared services and redistributes income. Major Commonwealth taxes: **income tax** (progressive, with higher rates on higher incomes), **GST** of 10% on most goods and services, **company tax** on business profits, plus excises and customs duties. **State taxes** include payroll, stamp duty, gambling and motor-vehicle taxes. **Local councils** collect rates.

Spending categories include social security and welfare (largest), health, education, defence, infrastructure, justice. The Australian Tax Office (ATO) administers federal tax; **PAYG** withholds tax from wages and reconciles at year-end.

**Worked example.** A Melbourne worker earns $1,200 a week. Their employer withholds tax through PAYG and forwards it to the ATO. Take-home is lower than gross. At year-end, the worker lodges a tax return. The ATO reconciles: refund if over-paid, bill if under-paid.

**Cultural context.** Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander-owned businesses pay company tax like others, and Aboriginal-led not-for-profits often deliver tax-funded services. Migrants pay tax from arrival and use tax-funded services like schools, hospitals and settlement programs. Specific cultural knowledge should only be read through published, community-approved material.

## Memorable takeaways
- Tax funds shared services.
- Income tax is progressive.
- GST is flat at 10%.
- Three levels of government, shared services.

## Watch out for these traps
- "GST is unfair because everyone pays it." Lower-income households pay a larger share of income on GST; concessions adjust the impact.
- "All my tax goes to roads." Social security is usually the largest single category.
- "Tax avoidance is the same as tax evasion." Avoidance is legal planning; evasion is illegal.

## Model responses

Use these to check your thinking after you have had a go.

- *"Progressive" tax.* Higher-income earners pay a higher percentage on additional dollars.
- *Why services span government levels.* Different scales fund different parts; cooperation is part of the system.
- *Social-security example.* Age pension; family tax benefit; JobSeeker; disability support.
- *Why tax morale matters.* Compliance is voluntary in large part; perceived fairness drives willingness to comply.
- *Short summary of PAYG and a tax return.* Employer withholds tax weekly; ATO reconciles at year-end via a tax return; refund or bill depending on whether withholding matched final liability.
