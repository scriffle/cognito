- **Big ideas:** Humans evolved in Africa and spread worldwide. Sahul was reached by sea. Interpretations evolve with evidence.

- You should be able to read deep history as evidence and interpretation.

## Core content

Modern humans (Homo sapiens) evolved in Africa around 300,000 years ago. From about 70,000 years ago, waves of Homo sapiens left Africa for Europe, Asia, Australia and the Americas.

During the last Ice Age, lower sea levels joined Australia, New Guinea and Tasmania into **Sahul**; Asia and its northern islands formed **Sunda**; deep water lay between them in **Wallacea**. Reaching Sahul required seafaring.

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Peoples are among the longest continuous cultures on Earth. **Madjedbebe** (Mirarr Country, NT) dates occupation to at least **65,000 years ago**. **Lake Mungo** (Paakantji, Ngyiampaa and Mutthi Mutthi Country) documents ceremonial burials from at least 40,000 years ago. Across Sahul, distinct nations developed languages, lore, songlines, technologies and economies adapted to varied environments.

**Worked example: Lake Mungo.** Mungo Lady (cremated burial) and Mungo Man (red-ochre burial) are among the world's earliest documented ceremonial burials. Discovered 1968–1974, remains were returned to community.

Interpretations evolve as new dating, DNA analysis and Aboriginal-led research arrive. Single-wave stories have given way to multiple-route, multiple-time accounts.

**Cultural context.** Many First Nations describe existence on Country since the Dreaming, not via migration. Both archaeological dating and community accounts are taught together. Specific cultural knowledge should only be read through published, community-approved material.

## Memorable takeaways
- Humans evolved in Africa.
- Sahul was reached by sea.
- 65,000+ years of continuous culture.
- Many nations, many languages.

## Watch out for these traps
- "Humans evolved from chimps." We share an ancestor.
- "Aboriginal arrival is recent." Evidence points to 65,000+ years.
- "First Nations is one culture." Hundreds of distinct nations.

## Model responses

Use these to check your thinking after you have had a go.

- *Why dating refines.* New techniques and sites change interpretations.
- *Why Sahul reflects seafaring.* Deep water always separated Sunda from Sahul.
- *Madjedbebe's significance.* 65,000+ years pushes arrival far earlier than older estimates.
- *Why "one culture" is wrong.* Many nations, languages and lore developed across Sahul.
- *Short summary of deep history.* Humans evolved in Africa, spread worldwide, reached Sahul 65,000+ years ago, and established many distinct First Nations cultures still continuous today.
