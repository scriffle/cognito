## Core content

**1. Beliefs and values overview.** Across diverse Nations, common values recur: **custodial responsibility** to Country; **reciprocity** with kin and Country; **respect for Elders and Lore**; **truthful speaking**; **patience and time-depth**; **collective wellbeing**. *Quick check:* Why is "custodial" different from "ownership"?

**2. Country and Place.** Daily decisions about food, ceremony, work and travel respect Country. Welcome to Country and Acknowledgement of Country are public expressions. *Quick check:* What is the difference between Welcome and Acknowledgement?

**3. Land use values.** Knowledge of seasons (e.g., Gariwerd six-season calendar; D'harawal six-season); fire-stick farming; protecting sacred sites; harvesting in season. *Quick check:* Why is "season" more detailed than four seasons?

**4. Trade values.** Long-distance trade (ochre, pearl-shell, stone-axe heads, fibres) was woven with ceremony and protocol. Trading partners were also ceremonial partners. *Quick check:* Why was trade about more than goods?

**5. Technology values.** Tools (grinding stones, fish-traps, spear-throwers, fibre nets) were designed for sustainable use across generations. Innovation was tested by Lore. *Quick check:* Why is "sustainable design" embedded in the values?

**6. Story values.** Stories transmit Lore, ecology, kinship, geography and history. They are taught, performed and lived — and may be restricted by Lore. *Quick check:* Why do stories teach more than morals?

**7. Worked example: Wurundjeri Woi Wurrung values today.** Welcome to Country; cultural-heritage protection along Birrarung (Yarra); language reclamation through the Wurundjeri Woi Wurrung Cultural Heritage Aboriginal Corporation; partnership with Parks Victoria. *Follow the thinking:* values past and present in one Country.

## Watch out for these traps
- "Values are abstract." They are practiced daily.
- "All Nations share the same values." Common themes, distinct expressions.
- "Welcome and Acknowledgement are the same." They are not; only Traditional Owners give Welcome.
- "Story is just entertainment." It carries Lore.

## Model responses

Use these to check your own thinking after you have had a go.

**Quick checks (Core content)**

1. *Custody vs ownership.* Custody is ongoing responsibility under Lore; ownership is a market right.
2. *Welcome vs Acknowledgement.* Welcome is by Traditional Owners; Acknowledgement is by visitors recognising Country.
3. *Why seasons are more detailed.* Country shows more than four states — calendars track plants, animals, water and weather together.
4. *Why trade is more than goods.* It is also ceremony, kinship and obligation.
5. *Sustainable design in values.* Tools and harvesting must protect Country for future generations.
6. *Why stories teach more than morals.* They encode law, ecology, geography and identity.

**Try these scenarios**
