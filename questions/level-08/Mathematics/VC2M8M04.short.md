# VC2M8M04 — Mathematics (Short)

## Snapshot
- **Descriptor:** Solve problems involving time and duration, including using 12- and 24-hour time across multiple time zones.
- **Year / Area:** Years 7–8, Mathematics.
- **Big ideas:** Time uses base 60, not base 10. 12-hour and 24-hour formats represent the same moments. Time zones mean one instant has different clock readings in different places.

## What you are learning
- You are learning to **solve problems involving time, duration, 12/24-hour conversions and time zones**.
- You should be able to calculate elapsed time, convert formats, and schedule across zones.

## Core content

**12-hour ↔ 24-hour.** Add 12 to pm hours: 3:27 pm = 15:27. Midnight = 00:00. Noon = 12:00.

**Duration.** End time − start time. Use base 60: 14:50 − 09:15 = 5 h 35 min. Borrow 60 minutes when needed.

**Across midnight.** Split: time to midnight + time after. 22:30 to 02:15 = 1 h 30 min + 2 h 15 min = 3 h 45 min.

**Adding/subtracting intervals.** 2 h 45 min + 1 h 30 min = 3 h 75 min = 4 h 15 min. Convert when minutes exceed 60.

**Australian time zones.** AEST (UTC+10, Vic), ACST (UTC+9:30, SA/NT), AWST (UTC+8, WA). Daylight saving: AEDT = UTC+11. Melbourne 3:00 pm AEDT = Perth 12:00 pm AWST (3 hours behind).

**Worked example.** Video call at 4:00 pm Melbourne (AEDT, UTC+11). Convert to UTC: 05:00. Tokyo (UTC+9): 14:00 ✓. London (UTC+0): 05:00 am ✗. Time-zone scheduling requires trade-offs.

**Victorian and cultural context.** Metro Trains Melbourne uses 24-hour timetables. The Australian Open schedules matches for global TV audiences across time zones. First Nations seasonal calendars mark time through environmental indicators (flowering, animal behaviour, star positions) — a different but valid approach to tracking cycles. Multicultural families navigate time zones daily for international calls.

## Memorable takeaways
- 24-hour: add 12 to pm hours; midnight = 00:00.
- Duration: subtract, borrow 60 if needed.
- Across midnight: split before and after, then add.
- Time zones: convert via UTC offsets.

## Watch out for these traps
- "Subtracted times like decimals." Minutes are base 60, not 100.
- "Forgot daylight saving." Melbourne shifts between UTC+10 and UTC+11 seasonally.
- "Midnight is 24:00." Standard: 00:00 starts the new day.

## Model responses

Use these to check your thinking after you have had a go.

- *3:27 pm → 24-hour.* 15:27.
- *Movie 7:40 pm to 10:05 pm.* Duration = 2 h 25 min.
- *Night shift 11 pm to 6:30 am.* 7 h 30 min.
- *3 h 50 min + 2 h 25 min.* 6 h 15 min.
- *Melbourne 9:00 am AEDT → Perth AWST.* 6:00 am (3 hours behind).
