# VC2M8N04 — Mathematics (Short)

## Snapshot
- **Descriptor:** Use the 4 operations with integers and with rational numbers, choosing and using efficient mental and written strategies, and digital tools where appropriate, and making estimates for these computations.
- **Year / Area:** Years 7–8, Mathematics (Number).
- **Big ideas:** Sign rules extend the four operations to negative numbers. Choosing between mental, written and digital strategies depends on the numbers. Estimation catches errors before they stick.

## What you are learning
- You are learning to **multiply, divide, add and subtract integers and rational numbers** (including negative fractions and decimals) and to **estimate** results.
- You should be able to apply BODMAS to expressions with negative values.

## Core content

**Sign rules for multiplication and division.** Same signs → positive. Different signs → negative. (−3) × (−4) = +12. (−5) × 2 = −10. (−20) ÷ (−4) = +5. (−18) ÷ 3 = −6.

**Negative fractions:** apply sign rules first, then fraction rules. (−2/3) × (3/4) = −6/12 = −1/2. (−3/5) ÷ (−1/2) = (−3/5) × (−2/1) = +6/5.

**Negative decimals:** calculate as if positive, then apply the sign. (−2.4) × (−1.5) = +3.6. (−7.2) ÷ 0.9 = −8.

**BODMAS with negatives.** (−3)² = 9 (square the whole thing: negative × negative). But −3² = −9 (square the 3, then negate). Brackets matter.

**Worked example.** (−1/2)² × 8 − (−3). Step 1: (−1/2)² = 1/4. Step 2: 1/4 × 8 = 2. Step 3: 2 − (−3) = 2 + 3 = 5.

**Estimation.** Round first: (−4.7) × 3.2 ≈ (−5) × 3 = −15. Exact: −15.04. The estimate confirms sign and magnitude.

## Memorable takeaways
- Same signs → positive; different signs → negative (for × and ÷).
- (−a)² is positive; −a² is negative. Brackets matter.
- BODMAS applies fully to negative numbers.
- Estimate first: check sign and magnitude before committing.

## Watch out for these traps
- Confusing (−3)² = 9 with −3² = −9. Brackets make the difference.
- Dropping negatives mid-calculation — write every sign explicitly.
- Not estimating — a quick mental check catches sign errors immediately.
