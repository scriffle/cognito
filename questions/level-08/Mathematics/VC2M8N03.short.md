# VC2M8N03 — Mathematics (Short)

## Snapshot
- **Descriptor:** Convert between fractions and terminating or recurring decimals, using digital tools as appropriate.
- **Year / Area:** Years 7–8, Mathematics (Number).
- **Big ideas:** Fractions and decimals are two forms of the same rational number. Terminating decimals have denominators with only factors of 2 and 5. Recurring decimals have other prime factors in the denominator.

## What you are learning
- You are learning to **convert fractions to terminating or recurring decimals** and **convert decimals back to fractions**, including using the algebraic method for recurring decimals.
- You should be able to predict whether a fraction will terminate or recur.

## Core content

**Fraction to decimal:** divide the numerator by the denominator. 3/8 = 0.375 (terminates). 1/3 = 0.3̇ (recurs). A fraction in simplest form **terminates** when its denominator has only prime factors of 2 and/or 5. It **recurs** when the denominator has any other prime factor.

**Dot notation** marks the repeating block: dots over the first and last repeating digits. 1/7 = 0.1̇4285̇7̇. 1/6 = 0.16̇ (only the 6 repeats).

**Terminating decimal to fraction:** use place value. 0.375 = 375/1000 = 3/8.

**Recurring decimal to fraction — algebraic method.** Convert 0.7̇2̇: let x = 0.7272…; 100x = 72.7272…; subtract: 99x = 72; x = 72/99 = 8/11.

**Worked example.** (a) 5/6 = 0.83̇. (b) 11/16: denominator 16 = 2⁴, only factor of 2, so it terminates. 11 ÷ 16 = 0.6875. (c) 0.1̇8̇: let x = 0.1818…; 100x = 18.18…; 99x = 18; x = 2/11.

**Victorian context.** MCG batting averages display fractions as rounded decimals. VicRoads fuel prices (178.9 c/L) represent fractions of a cent. Understanding these conversions helps students read statistical reports, including those published by the Yoorrook Justice Commission where data is presented as decimal percentages derived from fractional proportions. Specific cultural knowledge should only be read through published, community-approved material.

## Memorable takeaways
- Every fraction either terminates or recurs — no other option.
- Terminates: simplified denominator has only factors of 2 and/or 5.
- Recurs: denominator has any other prime factor.
- The algebraic method (multiply, subtract, solve) converts recurring decimals back to fractions.

## Watch out for these traps
- Placing dots incorrectly — in 0.16̇ only the 6 repeats, not the 1.
- Confusing "non-terminating" with "irrational" — recurring decimals are still rational.
- Rounding a recurring decimal and calling it exact — 1/3 ≠ 0.33.

## Model responses

- *7/40 terminate?* Yes (40 = 2³ × 5).
