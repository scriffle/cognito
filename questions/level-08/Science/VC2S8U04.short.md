# VC2S8U04 — Science Understanding (Short)

## Snapshot
- **Descriptor:** Matter and energy flow through ecosystems via food webs and food pyramids, and changes to any part of the system can affect the whole ecosystem.
- **Year / Area:** Year 8, Science.
- **Big ideas:** Energy flows one way. Matter cycles. Webs, not chains.

## What you are learning
- You are learning that matter and energy **flow** through ecosystems via **food webs** and **food pyramids**.
- You should be able to predict one knock-on effect of removing a species.

## Core content

**Producers** convert sunlight into chemical energy through photosynthesis. **Consumers** eat other organisms. **Decomposers** like bacteria and fungi break down dead matter and recycle nutrients. A **trophic level** is a step in the food chain. A **food pyramid** shows energy or biomass at each level. Energy is lost as heat at each step, so the pyramid narrows toward the top.

A **food web** is a connected network of food chains. Most species eat or are eaten by several others, so removing one species pulls multiple threads at once. Energy flows one direction: sunlight in, heat out. Matter (carbon, nitrogen, water) cycles.

**Worked example.** A Wadawurrung grassland near Ballarat. Producers: kangaroo grass and wallaby grass. Primary consumers: kangaroos, native bees, grasshoppers. Secondary consumers: brown falcons, snakes, magpies. Decomposers: fungi and dung beetles. Energy: sunlight → grass → kangaroo → falcon. Matter cycles between soil, plants, animals and droppings.

**Cultural context.** Wadawurrung, Wurundjeri Woi-wurrung, Gunaikurnai and other Victorian peoples have managed ecosystems through cool burning, water management and seasonal harvesting for thousands of years. Specific cultural knowledge should only be read through published, community-approved material.

## Memorable takeaways
- Sun in, heat out.
- Producers feed everything.
- Webs, not chains.
- Matter cycles, energy flows.

## Watch out for these traps
- "Energy is recycled in ecosystems." No. Energy flows one way: sunlight in, heat out.
- "Top predators are the most important." No. Producers and decomposers are equally critical.
- "Removing pests has no downsides." No. Even invasive species often have unexpected roles.

## Model responses

Use these to check your thinking after you have had a go.

- *Why does almost all life depend on producers?* Producers convert sunlight into chemical energy. Without them, no other trophic level has an energy supply.
- *Why do energy pyramids narrow at the top?* Energy is lost as heat at each level, so less is available to support fewer top consumers.
- *Why is "energy is recycled" wrong?* Energy leaves as heat and must be replaced by sunlight. It does not loop back.
- *Why is a single food chain misleading?* Most species eat several others. A chain hides links and overstates the impact of removing any one species.
- *Short trace from a magpie eating a worm.* Sunlight → plants → litter and microbes → worm → magpie. Matter cycles back to soil through droppings and decomposers.
