# VC2S8U15 — Science Understanding (Short)

## Snapshot
- **Descriptor:** Energy exists in different forms, can be transferred and transformed, and we can analyse how efficiently energy moves through a system.
- **Year / Area:** Year 8, Science.
- **Big ideas:** Energy comes in many forms. It moves and changes but never disappears. Efficiency = useful out / total in.

## What you are learning
- You are learning that **energy** exists in different **forms**, can be **transferred** and **transformed**, and that we can analyse **efficiency**.
- You should be able to track energy across a real device.

## Core content

**Energy** is the capacity to do work, measured in **joules**. Common forms: kinetic, gravitational potential, elastic, thermal, light, sound, chemical, electrical and nuclear. **Transfer** is energy moving in the same form, like heat from a pan to a hand. **Transformation** is energy changing form, like chemical to thermal in a candle.

The **law of conservation of energy** says energy is never created or destroyed. **Efficiency** = useful energy out ÷ total energy in. A modern LED is about 30% efficient; an incandescent bulb is about 5%. A **Sankey diagram** shows energy flows as arrows whose width matches the energy, making losses visible.

**Worked example.** A pedal-powered phone charger. Chemical energy in food → kinetic in legs → mechanical in pedals → electrical in dynamo → battery in phone. Some energy turns into heat in muscles, friction in gears and resistance in wires. The Sankey diagram shows useful charging energy as a narrow arrow and wasted heat as wider ones.

**Cultural context.** Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander practices of cool burning, food preparation and shelter design demonstrate energy-efficient management of Country. Specific cultural knowledge should only be read through published, community-approved material.

## Memorable takeaways
- Energy is the capacity to do work.
- It moves and changes form, never disappears.
- Transfer same form. Transform new form.
- Efficiency = useful out / total in.

## Watch out for these traps
- "Energy is created by power stations." No. They transform stored energy into electrical energy.
- "Efficiency over 100% is possible." No. That would break conservation of energy.
- "Heat is just wasted energy." Often, but heat itself is a form of energy and useful in heaters.

## Model responses

Use these to check your thinking after you have had a go.

- *What two forms does a stretched bow store?* Elastic potential energy, plus a small amount of gravitational potential if held high.
- *Sunlight warming a window: transfer or transformation?* Both. Light is transferred from Sun to window, then transformed into thermal energy.
- *Efficiency: 25 J useful from 100 J in.* 25%.
- *Where does a torch's chemical energy go after an hour?* Mostly transformed into light and heat that is dissipated to the surroundings.
- *Short summary of the pedal charger.* Energy flows from food chemical to muscle motion to pedal mechanical to dynamo electrical to phone battery, with heat lost at each step.
