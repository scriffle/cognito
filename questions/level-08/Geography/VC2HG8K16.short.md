## Core content

Geomorphological hazards include **earthquakes**, **landslides**, **tsunamis** and **volcanic eruptions**. Causes: plate tectonics (earthquakes, volcanoes); rainfall and slope (landslides); underwater earthquakes (many tsunamis). Human factors increase vulnerability. Four phases: mitigation, preparedness, response, recovery.

Australia has moderate intra-plate earthquake risk; landslide risk in steep terrain; tsunami risk on parts of the coast. Climate change adds compounding effects: heavier rain triggers more landslides; fires followed by storms cause mudflows.

**Worked example.** The 2021 Mansfield earthquake. Magnitude 5.9 in north-east Victoria; felt across the state. Cause: fault movement deep below the Earth's surface. Impacts: building damage in Melbourne CBD (Chapel Street); injuries; widespread disruption; aftershocks for months. Responses: emergency activation, building inspections, code discussions, ongoing monitoring by Geoscience Australia.

**Cultural context.** Oral histories in Australia record ancient flooding and volcanic events. Aboriginal-led cultural burning reduces compounding hazard risks. Multilingual emergency communications improved after major events. Specific cultural knowledge should only be read through published, community-approved material.

## Memorable takeaways
- Hazard plus exposure equals disaster.
- Many types, many causes.
- Four phases.
- Climate change adds compounding effects.

## Watch out for these traps
- "Australia has no earthquakes." It does; sometimes significant.
- "Disaster scale depends on the hazard alone." Exposure and vulnerability matter.
- "Aboriginal stories of earth movements are myth." Many are historical observations.

## Model responses

Use these to check your thinking after you have had a go.

- *Why landslides after fires.* Vegetation loss reduces soil stability; heavy rain washes weakened slopes.
- *Why mitigation saves more lives.* Prevents harm; rapid response only manages what happens.
- *Why Australia is less quake-prone than Japan.* Australia sits within a plate; Japan sits on multiple plate boundaries.
- *Fire-rain compounding example.* Black Summer 2019–20 followed by heavy rain caused mudflows.
- *Short summary of the Mansfield earthquake.* Fault movement caused damage and disruption; response combined emergency action, monitoring and review of building practices.
