# VC2HG8S02 — Geography (Short)

## Snapshot
- **Descriptor:** Collect, organise and process information and data from primary and secondary sources, including fieldwork, and using geospatial technologies and digital tools as appropriate.
- **Year / Area:** Year 8, Geography.
- **Big ideas:** Fieldwork is direct observation. Geospatial tools place data in space. Organisation makes analysis possible.

## What you are learning
- You are learning to **collect, organise and process** information and data from **primary and secondary sources**, including **fieldwork** and **geospatial tools**.
- You should be able to build a working evidence base.

## Core content

**Primary** data: collected from the original source (fieldwork, ABS Census, BoM data). **Secondary**: someone else's interpretation. **Fieldwork** is direct observation: counting, mapping, sketching, photographing, surveying. **GIS** layers data on maps; tools include Google Maps, Google Earth, ArcGIS Online, QGIS, Atlas of Living Australia.

Plan fieldwork: question, location, method, tools, recording, safety, ethics. Organise data with consistent headers (date, location, value, source, notes); save raw and cleaned versions; document method so others can repeat.

**Worked example.** A walkability study around your school. Question: how walkable is the 1 km radius? Method: map and walk transects; record sidewalk condition, tree cover, crossings; survey ten students. Tools: Google Maps, phone camera, spreadsheet. Output: labelled map with summary.

**Cultural context.** Cultural mapping projects led by Traditional Owners increase Indigenous spatial knowledge in public planning. Multilingual fieldwork helps collect data from diverse communities accurately. Specific cultural knowledge should only be read through published, community-approved material.

## Memorable takeaways
- Fieldwork is direct observation.
- Geospatial tools place data in space.
- Organisation makes analysis possible.
- Ethics and safety matter.

## Watch out for these traps
- "Memory is enough." Records are essential.
- "GIS is for experts only." Free tools are accessible.
- "Fieldwork is just walking around." Plan and record matter.

## Model responses

Use these to check your thinking after you have had a go.

- *Why is fieldwork primary?* You collect original observations.
- *GIS vs paper map.* GIS layers and updates; paper shows one snapshot.
- *Risk of overwriting raw data.* Errors become permanent; earlier insights lost.
- *Ethical issue in surveying classmates.* Consent; anonymity; privacy.
- *Short summary of the walkability study.* Mapped 1 km radius, transect walks, student survey, organised spreadsheet, labelled map with safe and ethical conduct.
