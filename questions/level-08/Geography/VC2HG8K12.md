## Core content

**1. Landscape vs landform.** A **landscape** is the overall surface form of an area (a coast, a mountain range, a desert). A **landform** is a specific feature (a beach, a peak, a sand dune). Many landforms make a landscape. *Quick check:* What is one landform that defines a coastal landscape?

**2. Major Australian landscape types.** Coastal; riverine; mountain; arid (desert); karst (limestone caves and sinkholes); volcanic; tropical (rainforest, savanna); aeolian (wind-shaped). Each has signature processes and landforms. *Quick check:* Which type covers most of central Australia?

**3. Coastal landscapes.** Features: beaches, cliffs, headlands, estuaries, dunes, reefs, lagoons. Distribution: along the entire Australian coastline; major examples include the Great Ocean Road (Victoria), Twelve Apostles, Great Barrier Reef. *Quick check:* What is the difference between a beach and a headland?

**4. Worked example: Victorian landscapes.** Volcanic plains (Western Volcanic Province): basalt plains and cones from recent volcanism. Coastal cliffs: Twelve Apostles formed by erosion of limestone. Mountain: Australian Alps in north-east Victoria. Riverine: Murray and tributaries. Each region shows different landforms shaped by different processes. *Follow the thinking:* identify region → name landscape type → list landforms → describe formation.

**5. Arid landscapes.** Features: sand dunes, salt lakes, gibber plains, eroded mountain ranges (Macdonnell, Flinders, Hamersley), inland drainage. Distribution: central and western Australia covers most of the continent's arid zone. *Quick check:* Why is "desert" not always "sand"?

**6. Karst landscapes.** Karst features form in soluble rock (limestone): caves, sinkholes, underground rivers. Examples: Nullarbor (WA/SA); Buchan (Victoria); Naracoorte (SA). *Quick check:* What gives karst its distinctive features?

**7. Distribution patterns.** Climate, geology and time shape distribution. Coasts wrap the continent; arid centre; tropical north; temperate south-east; alpine in highlands. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Peoples have detailed knowledge of every landscape type. *Quick check:* Why is "Australia is one type" wrong?

## Victorian and cultural context

- **Victorian anchor.** Victoria includes Alps (north-east), coastal cliffs (south-west), volcanic plains (west), riverine plains (north), tablelands and karst (Buchan, Gippsland). Parks Victoria manages many.
- **Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander perspective.** Traditional Owners across Victoria — Wurundjeri Woi-wurrung, Wadawurrung, Gunditjmara, Gunaikurnai, Yorta Yorta, Dja Dja Wurrung, Eastern Maar, Bunurong/Boon Wurrung — have detailed landscape knowledge built over millennia. Specific cultural knowledge should only be read through published, community-approved material.
- **Multicultural Australian perspective.** Migrant communities arriving from many landscapes (Mediterranean, Himalayan, Pacific, tropical) bring different landscape literacies. Australian landscapes can feel familiar or alien depending on background.

## Watch out for these traps
- "Desert = sand." Arid landscapes have many forms.
- "Landscape is just scenery." It is a system shaped by processes.
- "Distribution is fixed." Long-term geological change reshapes landscapes.
- "First Nations knowledge is folklore." It is detailed and scientifically valuable.

## Model responses

Use these to check your own thinking after you have had a go.

**Quick checks (Core content)**

1. *Coastal landform.* Beach; cliff; headland; estuary; dune; reef; lagoon.
2. *Central Australia dominant landscape.* Arid (desert).
3. *Beach vs headland.* Beach: low-lying, sandy or pebble shore. Headland: high promontory of rock projecting into the sea.
4. *Why "desert is not always sand".* Arid landscapes include gibber plains, salt lakes, rocky ranges and eroded plateaus.
5. *Karst features formed by.* Dissolution of limestone by slightly acidic water over long timescales.
6. *Why "Australia is one type" is wrong.* It hosts coastal, arid, alpine, tropical, karst, volcanic and riverine landscapes.
