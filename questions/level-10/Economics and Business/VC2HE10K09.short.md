# VC2HE10K09 — Economics and Business (Short)

## Snapshot
- **Descriptor:** Superannuation, what it is, who contributes, its importance and the potential benefits of superannuation to both individual and Australian living standards.
- **Year / Area:** Year 10, Economics and Business.
- **Big ideas:** Super is forced retirement saving. Compounding rewards early contributions. Choices shape later living standards.

## What you are learning
- You are learning about **superannuation**: what it is, who contributes, and its **benefits**.
- You should be able to make the most of compounding over decades.

## Core content

**Superannuation** is long-term savings for retirement. Employers pay the **Superannuation Guarantee (SG)** — 12% of ordinary-time earnings from 1 July 2025. Employees can add **voluntary or salary-sacrifice contributions**. The government adds co-contributions for low-income earners. Super is invested; returns compound over time. Access is usually at age 60+.

Multiple accounts duplicate fees and insurance; consolidation reduces costs. Low-fee MySuper funds preserve more returns. Total Australian super assets exceed $3 trillion, reducing reliance on the age pension.

**Worked example.** A 17-year-old casual worker earning $300/week (20 hours). Employer pays 12% super: $36/week, about $1,870/year. Invested in a low-cost diversified fund, this grows substantially by age 65. Early actions: choose a low-fee fund, consolidate accounts, check insurance, track via myGov.

**Cultural context.** Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander workers often face super gaps due to interrupted employment. ICAN and the Indigenous Superannuation Working Group provide culturally appropriate support. Migrant workers may have super in multiple countries. Specific cultural knowledge should only be read through published, community-approved material.

## Memorable takeaways
- Super = retirement savings, mostly from employer.
- 12% from 1 July 2025.
- Compounding rewards early contributions.
- Fees and multiple accounts erode balances.

## Watch out for these traps
- "Super is for old people, not me." Early years matter most due to compounding.
- "All super funds are the same." Fees, returns and options vary.
- "Super is locked away forever." Access at preservation age, usually 60.
