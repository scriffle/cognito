# VC2HE10K05 — Economics and Business (Short)

## Snapshot
- **Descriptor:** The strategies and tactics used by businesses to create and maintain a competitive advantage, including entrepreneurship, marketing and public relations.
- **Year / Area:** Year 10, Economics and Business.
- **Big ideas:** Advantage answers "why us, not them". Strategy plans; tactics test. PR earns; marketing pays.

## What you are learning
- You are learning the **strategies and tactics** businesses use to build **competitive advantage** through **entrepreneurship, marketing and PR**.
- You should be able to read why some businesses win.

## Core content

**Competitive advantage** is the reason customers choose one business over another: lower cost, better quality, faster service, unique features, brand trust, distribution, culture. **Strategy** is the long-term plan; **tactics** are specific moves. **Marketing** uses the 4Ps (product, price, place, promotion). **Public relations** manages reputation with customers, media, regulators and community.

Misleading marketing breaches the ACL. PR cannot rescue a bad product permanently. Greenwashing and dishonest claims erode long-term advantage.

**Worked example.** A Melbourne specialty-tea brand. Strategy: serve health-conscious customers with premium ethically sourced loose-leaf tea. Tactics: bright packaging, café partnerships, social-media campaigns, demos at Queen Victoria Market. PR: media stories on ethical sourcing; community events. Measures: brand awareness, repeat-purchase rate, NPS.

**Cultural context.** Aboriginal-owned businesses build advantage through cultural authority, community trust and reinvested profit (Clothing the Gaps, Welcome to Country). Migrant businesses across Melbourne build advantage through community-language access and culturally specific products. Specific cultural knowledge should only be read through published, community-approved material.

## Memorable takeaways
- Advantage answers "why us, not them".
- Strategy plans; tactics test.
- 4Ps: product, price, place, promotion.
- PR earns; marketing pays.

## Watch out for these traps
- "More advertising fixes it." Magnifies the wrong message if strategy is off.
- "Lowest price always wins." Not if quality, trust or service matter.
- "Greenwashing is harmless." It misleads customers and is increasingly regulated.

## Model responses

Use these to check your thinking after you have had a go.

- *Why is "we're the only one" fragile?* Competition catches up; advantage needs reinforcement.
- *Why is "advertise more" a tactic, not a strategy?* It does not name customer, position or measure.
- *Cheap experiment.* Pre-sell a small batch; run a one-week pop-up; test a single-channel ad.
- *Why do crises test PR?* Reputation is built over time and tested in stress.
- *Short summary of the specialty-tea strategy.* Clear customer, ethical positioning, 4Ps in action, PR through earned media, and measures that track repeat purchase and brand awareness.
