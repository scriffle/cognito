# VC2HE10K06 — Economics and Business (Short)

## Snapshot
- **Descriptor:** The use of human resource management to manage and motivate employees and improve business competitiveness.
- **Year / Area:** Year 10, Economics and Business.
- **Big ideas:** HR is people-strategy. Fair pay floors; growth motivates. Culture compounds.

## What you are learning
- You are learning how **HR management** motivates employees and improves **business competitiveness**.
- You should be able to see HR as the work of building a team that performs.

## Core content

**HRM** covers recruiting, training, paying, supporting and developing people. Key activities: clear job descriptions and bias-aware selection; structured onboarding; training and development; performance feedback; wellbeing support. **Motivation models** include Maslow (hierarchy), Herzberg (hygiene vs motivators) and Self-determination (autonomy, competence, relatedness). Fair pay is a floor; growth, recognition and meaning motivate.

**Retention** reduces hiring costs and builds capability. Diverse, inclusive cultures perform better in many studies. Good HR is strategic, not just administrative.

**Worked example.** A Melbourne café chain. Strategy: consistent quality at six locations. HR practices: structured hiring, two-day onboarding, weekly skill cards, monthly team meetings, profit-share, paid training time. Result: lower turnover, higher customer satisfaction, repeat business.

**Cultural context.** Reconciliation Action Plans and Aboriginal-employment strategies support First Nations workforce participation. Diverse workforce strategies, multilingual support, faith-aware leave policies and culturally inclusive onboarding help businesses across Melbourne. Specific cultural knowledge should only be read through published, community-approved material.

## Memorable takeaways
- HR is people-strategy.
- Recruitment, training, retention, culture.
- Culture compounds.
- Good HR is competitive advantage.

## Watch out for these traps
- "HR is admin only." Modern HR is strategic.
- "Pay alone solves morale." It does not.
- "Diversity is decoration." Inclusive culture drives performance.

## Model responses

Use these to check your thinking after you have had a go.

- *HR more than payroll.* Hiring, onboarding, training, performance, wellbeing, exit, culture.
- *Cost of a poor hire.* Onboarding cost, lost productivity, team morale, replacement cost.
- *Why fair pay matters.* It removes a major dissatisfaction so other motivators can land.
- *Strong-culture signal.* Low turnover, honest feedback, pride in work, visible inclusion.
- *Short summary of the café-chain HR strategy.* Structured hiring, onboarding and training, regular feedback and profit-share build consistency, lower turnover and better customer outcomes.
