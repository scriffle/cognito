# VC2HE10K08 — Economics and Business (Short)

## Snapshot
- **Descriptor:** Managing consumer and financial risks and rewards, including financial scams, smart investment strategies and insurance, considering both legal and ethical implications.
- **Year / Area:** Year 10, Economics and Business.
- **Big ideas:** Risk and reward go together. Diversify what you can. Verify licensing first.

## What you are learning
- You are learning to **manage consumer and financial risks and rewards**, including **scams**, **smart investing** and **insurance**.
- You should be able to make decisions that fit your life.

## Core content

**Risk** is the chance of loss; **reward** is the gain you accept the risk to chase. Higher reward usually means higher risk. Common risks: market, credit, inflation, liquidity, fraud. **Diversification** spreads risk by holding different assets. **Insurance** spreads risk across many policy-holders; types include health, contents, car, life, income protection.

Scams exploit urgency, fear and trust. Verify licensing through ASIC's professional register. Legal duties: disclose conflicts; provide accurate information; meet ACL and financial-services law. Ethical: do not push products beyond need; avoid greenwashing.

**Worked example.** A 17-year-old worker. Emergency fund $1,000 in high-interest savings. Medium-term car savings in safe deposit. Long-term goal in a low-cost diversified index fund. Insurance: think about health, contents and (later) income protection. Salary-sacrifice into super once working full-time.

**Cultural context.** The Indigenous Consumer Assistance Network (ICAN) supports culturally appropriate advice. Multilingual financial-counselling services support diverse Melbourne communities. Faith-based considerations (e.g., shariah-compliant finance) may apply. Specific cultural knowledge should only be read through published, community-approved material.

## Memorable takeaways
- Risk and reward go together.
- Diversify what you can.
- Insurance spreads risk.
- Legal and ethical both count.

## Watch out for these traps
- "High returns are normal." Over time, high returns usually mean high risk.
- "Insurance is a waste if I do not claim." It is risk-sharing.
- "Legal = ethical." Some legal products are exploitative.

## Model responses

Use these to check your thinking after you have had a go.

- *"Guaranteed high returns".* Scam signal; no legitimate product can guarantee this.
- *Cash and inflation.* Buying power falls over decades; inflation risk applies.
- *Insured vs guaranteed.* Insurance pays under defined events; guarantee promises a specific result.
- *"Legal but unethical".* Pushing high-fee products to people who do not need them.
- *Short summary of a young investor's plan.* Emergency fund in savings, diversified index fund for longer term, appropriate insurance, super top-up later; review yearly.
