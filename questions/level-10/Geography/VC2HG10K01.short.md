# VC2HG10K01 — Geography (Short)

## Snapshot
- **Descriptor:** The distribution and characteristics of biomes as regions with distinctive climates, vegetation and biomass productivity, and the potential to produce food.
- **Year / Area:** Year 10, Geography.
- **Big ideas:** Biomes are major living regions. Climate and soil shape them. Productivity sets food potential.

## What you are learning
- You are learning the **distribution and characteristics of biomes** and their **food-production potential**.
- You should be able to read the planet as a system of biomes.

## Core content

A **biome** is a large region with a particular climate, vegetation and animal life: tropical rainforest, savanna, desert, temperate forest, grassland, boreal forest, tundra, freshwater and marine. Temperature and rainfall are the major drivers. **Net primary productivity (NPP)** measures how fast plants build **biomass**. **Arable land** is suitable for crops; about 11% of Earth's land is arable. Marine biomes contribute 15–20% of human animal protein.

Climate change is shifting biome distributions: boreal forests creeping north, grasslands drying, coral biomes under heat stress.

**Worked example.** Australia's biomes. Tropical rainforest in north Queensland; savanna across the north; grasslands and woodlands in central regions; arid deserts in the interior; temperate forest in the south-east; alpine in the Victorian Alps; marine off all coasts. Each has distinctive vegetation and food potential.

**Cultural context.** First Nations knowledge of Country includes biome management: cool burning, eel aquaculture at Budj Bim, yam-daisy and grain-grass harvesting. Migrant farmers bring biome-specific knowledge. Specific cultural knowledge should only be read through published, community-approved material.

## Memorable takeaways
- Biomes are major living regions.
- Climate and soil shape characteristics.
- Productivity sets food potential.
- Climate change shifts boundaries.

## Watch out for these traps
- "Biome = ecosystem." Biomes are larger.
- "High NPP = high food production." Rainforests are highly productive but poor for agriculture.
- "Marine doesn't count." It is major for protein supply.
