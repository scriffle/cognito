# VC2M10AST01 — Mathematics (Short)

## Snapshot
- **Descriptor:** Calculate and interpret mean and standard deviation of data and use these to compare data sets.
- **Year / Area:** Years 9–10 Advanced, Mathematics.
- **Big ideas:** Mean measures centre; standard deviation measures spread around the mean. Comparing data sets requires both centre and spread. Outliers inflate both statistics.

## What you are learning
- You are learning to **calculate and interpret mean and standard deviation** and to use these to compare data sets.
- You should be able to explain what standard deviation reveals about consistency and variability.

## Core content

**Mean:** x̄ = Σxᵢ/n. For {4, 7, 8, 10, 11}: x̄ = 40/5 = 8.

**Deviation:** xᵢ − x̄. Deviations sum to zero. Square them before averaging.

**Standard deviation:** σ = √(Σ(xᵢ − x̄)²/n). For {4, 7, 8, 10, 11}: σ = √(30/5) = √6 ≈ 2.45. Sample SD uses n − 1.

**Interpretation:** Small SD → consistent (clustered near mean). Large SD → variable (spread out).

**Comparing:** Report both mean and SD. Higher mean + lower SD = consistently better performance.

**Outliers:** Extreme values inflate mean and SD. Consider median and IQR when outliers are present.

**Victorian context.** Quality control in Victorian manufacturing uses SD to monitor consistency. Karl Pearson (1894) developed the concept of standard deviation.

## Memorable takeaways
- Mean = Σxᵢ/n — the balance point.
- SD measures typical distance from the mean.
- Small SD → consistent; large SD → variable.
- Compare data sets using both mean and SD.
- Outliers inflate both mean and SD.

## Watch out for these traps
- Forgetting to square deviations (they sum to zero otherwise).
- Dividing by n (population) vs n − 1 (sample) — check which is required.
- Comparing SDs without context (SD of 5 means different things for different scales).

## Model responses

- *Mean of {12, 15, 18, 20, 25}.* 90/5 = 18.
- *SD of {3, 5, 7, 9, 11}.* σ = √(40/5) = √8 ≈ 2.83.
- *Data set A (SD 2) vs B (SD 8).* A is more consistent.
- *Class X (mean 72, SD 5) vs Y (mean 68, SD 12).* X performs better and more consistently.
- *Machines A and B, both mean 500.* A (SD ≈ 1.41) far more consistent than B (SD ≈ 7.72).
- *Outlier in {20, 22, 21, 23, 20, 100}.* Median (21.5) better represents centre than mean.
