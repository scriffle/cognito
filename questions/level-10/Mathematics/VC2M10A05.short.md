# VC2M10A05 — Mathematics (Short)

## Snapshot
- **Descriptor:** Substitute values into formulas to determine an unknown and rearrange formulas to solve for a particular term.
- **Year / Area:** Years 9–10, Mathematics.
- **Big ideas:** A formula connects variables — substituting known values finds the unknown. Rearranging uses inverse operations in reverse order. Any variable can become the subject.

## What you are learning
- You are learning to **substitute into formulas** and **rearrange to change the subject**.
- You should handle squares, square roots and fractions confidently.

## Core content

**Substitution.** Replace variables with values, then evaluate. v = u + at: if u = 12, a = 3, t = 5 → v = 27.

**Solving after substitution.** If the unknown is not the subject, substitute known values then solve. v = 30, u = 10, a = 4 in v = u + at: 30 = 10 + 4t → t = 5.

**Rearranging (changing the subject).** Use inverse operations in reverse. From v = u + at → t = (v − u)/a. Steps: subtract u, divide by a.

**With squares/roots.** E = ½mv² → v = √(2E/m). Steps: ×2, ÷m, √.

**With fractions.** R = V/I → I = V/R. Lens formula 1/f = 1/u + 1/v → v = fu/(u − f) (multiply through by fuv to clear fractions).

**Worked example.** V/Line: d = st, d = 110 km, t = 1.5 h. Rearrange: s = d/t = 110/1.5 ≈ 73.3 km/h. Check: 73.3 × 1.5 = 110 ✓.

**Always check** by substituting into both original and rearranged forms.

**Victorian and cultural context.** VicRoads uses v² = u² + 2as for stopping distances. Melbourne Water rearranges flow formulas to find pipe dimensions. Financial formulas (I = PRT, compound interest) are used by families across all backgrounds for savings and loan decisions.

## Memorable takeaways
- Substitute: replace variables with values, evaluate.
- Rearrange: undo operations in reverse (inverse operations).
- Squares → √. Multiply → ÷. Add → subtract.
- Always check by substituting back.

## Watch out for these traps
- "Forgot BODMAS after substituting." Powers before multiplication.
- "Lost a negative sign when rearranging." Track signs at every step.
- "Didn't check." One wrong operation contaminates everything — substituting back catches it.

## Model responses

Use these to check your thinking after you have had a go.

- *A = πr², r = 4.* A = 16π ≈ 50.27.
- *P = 2l + 2w; P = 56, l = 18.* w = 10.
- *A = ½bh → h = ?* h = 2A/b.
- *R = V/I → I = ?* I = V/R.
- *F = 9C/5 + 32 → C = ?* C = 5(F − 32)/9.
