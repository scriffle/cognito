# VC2M10AST02 — Mathematics (Short)

## Snapshot
- **Descriptor:** Describe the usefulness of different measures of spread and their effect on the interpretation of data for different distributions.
- **Year / Area:** Years 9–10 Advanced, Mathematics.
- **Big ideas:** Range, IQR and SD serve different purposes. IQR resists outliers; SD does not. Choice of measure affects conclusions drawn from data.

## What you are learning
- You are learning to **compare measures of spread** — range, IQR and standard deviation — and to determine which is most appropriate for a given distribution.
- You should be able to explain how the choice of spread measure affects interpretation.

## Core content

**Range:** max − min. Simple but uses only two values. Heavily affected by outliers.

**IQR:** Q3 − Q1. Spread of the middle 50%. Resistant to outliers. Best for skewed data.

**SD:** Typical distance from the mean. Uses all data. Sensitive to outliers. Best for symmetric data.

**Choosing:** Symmetric, no outliers → SD. Skewed or outliers → IQR. Range gives the crudest picture.

**Effect on interpretation:** For {10, 12, 13, 14, 15, 60}: range = 50 (misleading), IQR = 2 (better), SD ≈ 17.8 (inflated). Same data, different stories.

**Victorian context.** Public health, education and urban planning in Victoria require choosing appropriate spread measures. Francis Galton (19th century) developed the IQR concept.

## Memorable takeaways
- Range = max − min — simple but crude.
- IQR = Q3 − Q1 — middle 50%, outlier-resistant.
- SD — typical distance from mean, sensitive to outliers.
- Symmetric → SD. Skewed/outliers → IQR.
- Choice of measure changes the story.

## Watch out for these traps
- Reporting only range — it is the least informative measure.
- Using SD for skewed data — SD is distorted by skewness.
- Confusing IQR (Q3 − Q1) with range (max − min).

## Model responses

- *Range of {20, 25, 30, 35, 100}.* 80 — inflated by outlier 100.
- *IQR of {2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18}.* Q1 = 6, Q3 = 14, IQR = 8.
- *Most affected by outliers.* Range, then SD. IQR is least affected.
- *Skewed income data.* IQR is most appropriate.
- *{5, 5, 5, 5, 5, 50}.* Range = 45 is misleading; data is concentrated at 5.
- *Right-skewed example.* House prices, personal income, insurance claims.
