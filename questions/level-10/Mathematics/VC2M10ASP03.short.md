# VC2M10ASP03 — Mathematics (Short)

## Snapshot
- **Descriptor:** Define sin(x), cos(x) and tan(x) using the unit circle and graph these trigonometric functions.
- **Year / Area:** Years 9–10 Advanced, Mathematics.
- **Big ideas:** Unit circle defines sin θ as y-coordinate, cos θ as x-coordinate. tan θ = sin θ/cos θ. Trig functions are periodic: sin and cos have period 360°, tan has period 180°.

## What you are learning
- You are learning to **define trigonometric functions using the unit circle** and to **graph y = sin(x), y = cos(x) and y = tan(x)**.
- You should be able to identify amplitude, period, key features, and signs of trig functions in each quadrant.

## Core content

**Unit circle:** Radius 1, centred at origin. Point at angle θ has coordinates (cos θ, sin θ). Extends trig beyond right-angled triangles.

**Exact values:** sin(30°) = ½, cos(30°) = √3/2. sin(45°) = cos(45°) = √2/2. sin(60°) = √3/2, cos(60°) = ½. tan(θ) = sin(θ)/cos(θ).

**ASTC rule:** All positive in Q1, Sin in Q2, Tan in Q3, Cos in Q4. Memory aid: All Stations To Central.

**Graphs:** sin: (0,0)→max 1 at 90°→(180,0)→min −1 at 270°→(360,0). cos: same shape shifted 90° left. tan: period 180°, asymptotes at 90°+180°n.

**Transformations:** y = a·sin(bx): amplitude = |a|, period = 360°/b.

**Victorian context.** Trig functions model waves, oscillations and circular motion. Aryabhata (5th century CE, India) produced the first known sine table.

## Memorable takeaways
- Unit circle: cos θ = x-coordinate, sin θ = y-coordinate.
- ASTC: All, Sin, Tan, Cos — positive in quadrants 1, 2, 3, 4.
- sin and cos: amplitude 1, period 360°. tan: no amplitude, period 180°.
- For y = a·sin(bx): amplitude |a|, period 360°/b.

## Watch out for these traps
- Forgetting quadrant signs: sin(210°) is negative (Q3).
- Confusing period of tan (180°) with sin/cos (360°).
- Wrong exact values: sin(60°) = √3/2, not ½.

## Model responses

- *Coordinates at 90°.* (cos 90°, sin 90°) = (0, 1).
- *tan(60°).* (√3/2)/(½) = √3.
- *sin(210°).* −½ (Q3, sin negative).
- *cos(300°).* ½ (Q4, cos positive).
- *sin(x) = 0.* At 0°, 180°, 360°.
- *y = 3cos(2x).* Amplitude = 3, period = 180°.
