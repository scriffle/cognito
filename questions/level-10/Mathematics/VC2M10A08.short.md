# VC2M10A08 — Mathematics (Short)

## Snapshot
- **Descriptor:** Solve linear inequalities and graph their solutions on a number line.
- **Year / Area:** Years 9–10, Mathematics.
- **Big ideas:** Inequalities solve like equations except — flip the sign when multiplying/dividing by a negative. The solution is a range, shown on a number line with open or closed circles.

## What you are learning
- You are learning to **solve linear inequalities** and **graph their solutions on a number line**.
- You should handle multi-step inequalities and the critical "flip" rule confidently.

## Core content

**Symbols.** < less than. > greater than. ≤ at most. ≥ at least.

**Solving.** Use inverse operations, same as equations. 3x + 4 > 13 → 3x > 9 → x > 3.

**Critical rule.** Multiplying or dividing by a **negative** reverses the sign. −3x > 12 → x < −4 (flip!). Why: multiplying by −1 reverses order on the number line.

**Multi-step.** Expand, collect, solve. 4(x−2) < 3x+5 → 4x−8 < 3x+5 → x < 13.

**Graphing.** Open circle (○) for strict < or > (endpoint excluded). Closed circle (●) for ≤ or ≥ (included). Shade the valid direction.

**Compound.** −1 < 2x+3 ≤ 9 → subtract 3: −4 < 2x ≤ 6 → divide by 2: −2 < x ≤ 3.

**Worked example.** Student with $80 at a festival: entry $25, food $5.50 each. 25 + 5.5n ≤ 80 → 5.5n ≤ 55 → n ≤ 10. At most 10 food items. Graph: closed circle at 10, shade left.

**Victorian and cultural context.** Speed limits (speed ≤ 60), WorkSafe noise thresholds (dB ≤ 85), Melbourne Water's minimum environmental flows — all real-world inequalities. Yorta Yorta water-rights negotiations set minimum river-flow levels below which cultural and ecological values are harmed. Financial literacy: "Can I afford this?" means expenses ≤ income.

## Memorable takeaways
- Solve like equations — but flip sign for negative multiplication/division.
- Solution is a range, not a single value.
- ○ for strict (< >). ● for inclusive (≤ ≥).
- Check whole-number constraints in context.

## Watch out for these traps
- "Forgot to flip when dividing by negative." The most common error.
- "Closed circle for strict inequality." x > 3 means 3 is NOT included — use open circle.
- "Solved for one value." Inequalities have infinitely many solutions.

## Model responses

Use these to check your thinking after you have had a go.

- *Solve 2x − 7 ≤ 11.* x ≤ 9.
- *Solve −2x + 6 ≥ 14.* x ≤ −4 (flip!).
- *Solve 5 − 2(x+1) > 7.* x < −2.
- *Solve −1 < 2x+3 ≤ 9.* −2 < x ≤ 3.
- *Guitar: 120 + 35w ≥ 450.* w ≥ 10 weeks (round up).
