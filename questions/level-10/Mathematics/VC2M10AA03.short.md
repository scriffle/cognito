# VC2M10AA03 — Mathematics (Short)

## Snapshot
- **Descriptor:** Simplify combinations of linear expressions with rational coefficients and solve equations with algebraic fractions.
- **Year / Area:** Years 9–10 Advanced, Mathematics.
- **Big ideas:** Rational coefficients follow the same algebraic rules as integers. Solving equations with algebraic fractions requires clearing denominators using the LCD. Solutions must be checked against excluded values.

## What you are learning
- You are learning to **simplify linear expressions with fractional coefficients** and to **solve equations containing algebraic fractions**.
- You should be able to find the LCD, clear denominators, solve, and verify solutions are valid.

## Core content

**Simplifying with rational coefficients:** Distribute and collect like terms. ⅔(6x − 9) + ¾(8x + 4) = 4x − 6 + 6x + 3 = 10x − 3.

**LCD of algebraic fractions:** The smallest expression divisible by each denominator. For denominators 3 and 4, the LCD is 12.

**Adding/subtracting algebraic fractions:** Convert to common denominator, combine numerators. (2x − 1)/3 − (x + 2)/4 = (5x − 10)/12 = 5(x − 2)/12.

**Solving:** Multiply every term by the LCD. For (2x − 1)/3 − (x + 2)/4 = 1: multiply by 12 → 5x − 10 = 12 → x = 22/5.

**Excluded values:** If a variable appears in a denominator, values making it zero are excluded from the domain.

**Rearranging formulas:** 1/f = 1/u + 1/v → f = uv/(u + v). The same LCD technique applies to formulas from physics and engineering.

**Victorian context.** The lens formula 1/f = 1/u + 1/v is a standard VCE Physics application. Brahmagupta (7th century CE) and Islamic mathematicians systematised algebraic fraction manipulation.

## Memorable takeaways
- Distribute fractional coefficients carefully to each term inside brackets.
- Multiply every term by the LCD — not just the fractions.
- Always check that solutions do not make any denominator zero.
- Only factors cancel in fractions, never individual terms.

## Watch out for these traps
- Multiplying only some terms by the LCD, leaving others unconverted.
- Sign errors when distributing a negative across a bracketed numerator.
- Cancelling terms instead of factors: (x + 3)/x ≠ 4.

## Model responses

Use these to check your thinking after you have had a go.

- *Simplify ½(4x + 6) − ⅓(9x − 3).* 2x + 3 − 3x + 1 = −x + 4.
- *LCD of (x + 1)/6 and (2x − 3)/4.* 12.
- *Simplify (3x + 1)/5 + (x − 4)/3.* (14x − 17)/15.
- *Solve (x + 3)/2 − (x − 1)/5 = 4.* x = 23/3.
- *Excluded values for 4/(x + 1) = 2/(x − 3).* x ≠ −1 and x ≠ 3.
- *Rearrange 1/R = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂.* R = R₁R₂/(R₁ + R₂).
