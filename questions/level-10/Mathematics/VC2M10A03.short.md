# VC2M10A03 — Mathematics (Short)

## Snapshot
- **Descriptor:** Apply the four operations to simple algebraic fractions with numerical or single variable denominators.
- **Year / Area:** Years 9–10, Mathematics.
- **Big ideas:** Algebraic fractions follow the same rules as numerical fractions. Find common denominators for +/−, multiply across for ×, flip and multiply for ÷. Simplify by cancelling common factors.

## What you are learning
- You are learning to **add, subtract, multiply and divide algebraic fractions** with numerical or single-variable denominators.
- You should find common denominators, simplify results, and work confidently with algebra in fraction form.

## Core content

An **algebraic fraction** has variables in the numerator, denominator, or both (e.g. x/3, 4/a, (2x+1)/5).

**Adding/subtracting — same denominator:** combine numerators directly. 5a/7 − 2a/7 = 3a/7.

**Different denominators:** find the **LCD**. x/3 + x/5: LCD = 15. Convert: 5x/15 + 3x/15 = 8x/15. For variable denominators: 2/a + 3/b: LCD = ab. Result: (2b + 3a)/(ab).

**Multiplying:** numerator × numerator, denominator × denominator, then cancel. (3a/4) × (8/a²) = 24a/(4a²) = 6/a.

**Dividing:** flip the second fraction and multiply. (6/a) ÷ (3/a²) = (6/a) × (a²/3) = 2a.

**Worked example.** Two pipes fill a tank: rates 1/x and 1/(2x) per hour. Together: 1/x + 1/(2x) = 2/(2x) + 1/(2x) = 3/(2x) per hour. Check with x = 4: 1/4 + 1/8 = 3/8 = 3/(2×4) ✓.

**Victorian context.** Parallel resistance (1/R = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂) and combined work rates use algebraic fractions. Engineers at CSIRO and the Australian Synchrotron simplify these daily.

## Memorable takeaways
- Same denominator: add/subtract numerators directly.
- Different denominators: find LCD, convert, combine.
- Multiply: across. Divide: flip and multiply.
- Cancel common **factors** only — never terms within a sum.

## Watch out for these traps
- Cancelling terms instead of factors: in (x + 3)/x, the x cannot be cancelled.
- Adding denominators: x/3 + x/5 ≠ x/8. Find the LCD.
- Forgetting to flip when dividing: ÷ means × reciprocal.

## Model responses

Use these to check your thinking after you have had a go.

- *5a/7 − 2a/7.* 3a/7.
- *x/4 + x/6.* LCD = 12. 3x/12 + 2x/12 = 5x/12.
- *4/m − 1/n.* (4n − m)/(mn).
- *(3a/4) × (8/a²).* 6/a.
- *(6/a) ÷ (3/a²).* 2a.
