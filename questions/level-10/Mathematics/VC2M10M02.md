## Core content

**1. Why logarithmic scales?** Some quantities span factors of millions or billions (earthquake energy, sound intensity, hydrogen-ion concentration). On a linear scale, small values would be invisible next to large ones. A **logarithmic (log) scale** assigns equally spaced markings to powers of 10: 1, 10, 100, 1 000, 10 000. Each step is ×10, not +10. *Quick check:* On a log scale, how far apart are 100 and 100 000?

**2. What is a logarithm (intuitive)?** log₁₀(x) answers: "10 to what power gives x?" log₁₀(100) = 2 because 10² = 100. log₁₀(1 000 000) = 6. Each whole-number increase in the log means the quantity is 10 times larger. *Quick check:* What is log₁₀(10 000)?

**3. The Richter scale (earthquakes).** Each whole-number increase represents roughly 10× more ground shaking and about 31.6× more energy released. A magnitude-7 earthquake has 10× the shaking of magnitude-6 and about 1 000× (31.6³) the energy of magnitude-4. *Quick check:* How many times more ground shaking does a magnitude-5 earthquake have compared to magnitude-3?

**4. The decibel scale (sound).** Sound intensity is measured in decibels (dB). Every 10 dB increase = 10× the intensity. A normal conversation is about 60 dB; a rock concert is about 110 dB — that is 10⁵ = 100 000 times more intense. *Quick check:* A lawn mower is 90 dB and a whisper is 30 dB. How many times more intense is the mower?

**5. The pH scale (acidity).** pH = −log₁₀[H⁺]. Each unit decrease in pH means 10× more hydrogen ions (more acidic). pH 3 is 10× more acidic than pH 4 and 100× more than pH 5. Pure water has pH 7 (neutral). *Quick check:* How many times more acidic is a solution with pH 2 compared to pH 5?

**6. Worked example: comparing earthquake magnitudes.**

The 2021 Melbourne earthquake was magnitude 5.9. The 2004 Boxing Day earthquake (Indian Ocean) was magnitude 9.1.

*Follow the thinking:*
- Difference in magnitude = 9.1 − 5.9 = 3.2.
- Ground-shaking ratio = 10^3.2 ≈ 1 585 times.
- Energy ratio ≈ 10^(1.5 × 3.2) = 10^4.8 ≈ 63 096 times.
- Interpretation: the Boxing Day earthquake released over 63 000 times more energy than Melbourne's 2021 event.

**7. Reading log-scale graphs.** On a log-scale axis, equal spaces represent equal ratios (not equal differences). A straight line on a log-linear graph means exponential growth. A straight line on a log-log graph means a power relationship. Be careful: visually "small" gaps on a log graph can represent enormous real differences. *Quick check:* On a log-scale graph, two data points are 2 units apart vertically. What is the actual ratio between them?

## Memorable takeaways
- Log scales: each step is ×10, not +10.
- log₁₀(x) = "10 to what power gives x?"
- Richter: +1 magnitude = 10× shaking, ~31.6× energy.
- Decibels: +10 dB = 10× intensity.
- pH: −1 unit = 10× more acidic.
- Small differences on a log scale hide enormous real-world differences.

## Watch out for these traps
- "A magnitude-6 earthquake is twice as strong as magnitude-3." On the Richter scale, the difference is 10³ = 1 000 times the shaking, not 2×. Log scales are multiplicative, not additive.
- "A 10 dB increase means 10% louder." It means 10 times the intensity — a massive increase, not a small one.
- "pH 3 is three times more acidic than pH 1." pH 3 is 10² = 100 times LESS acidic than pH 1. Lower pH = more acidic, and each unit is ×10.
- "Log scales distort the truth." They reveal patterns (exponential growth, power laws) that linear scales hide. Both scales are valid — choose the one that serves your purpose.
