# VC2M10AA09 — Mathematics (Short)

## Snapshot
- **Descriptor:** Solve linear and non-linear simultaneous equations using graphing or systematic methods.
- **Year / Area:** Years 9–10 Advanced, Mathematics.
- **Big ideas:** Non-linear simultaneous equations involve at least one non-linear equation. The discriminant determines 0, 1, or 2 intersection points. Substitution converts the system to a single quadratic equation.

## What you are learning
- You are learning to **solve simultaneous equations** where at least one is non-linear, using substitution and graphical methods.
- You should be able to classify intersections (secant, tangent, none) and set up real-world systems.

## Core content

**Linear-quadratic substitution:** y = x² and y = 2x + 3 → x² − 2x − 3 = 0 → (x − 3)(x + 1) = 0. Points: (3, 9) and (−1, 1).

**Circle-line systems:** x² + y² = 25 and y = x + 1 → 2x² + 2x − 24 = 0 → (x + 4)(x − 3) = 0. Points: (3, 4) and (−4, −3).

**Discriminant determines intersections:** Δ > 0 → secant (2 points); Δ = 0 → tangent (1 point); Δ < 0 → no intersection.

**Tangency condition:** y = 2x + k tangent to y = x² when Δ = 0: 4 + 4k = 0, so k = −1.

**Real-world setup:** Rectangle with area 60 m², perimeter 34 m → dimensions 12 × 5 via l² − 17l + 60 = 0.

**Victorian context.** Chinese "Nine Chapters on the Mathematical Art" (circa 200 BCE) included systematic methods for solving equation systems. Victorian engineers use these techniques for structural intersection problems.

## Memorable takeaways
- Substitute the linear equation into the non-linear one.
- The discriminant classifies: 2 points, tangent, or no intersection.
- Always find both coordinates — a solution is a point (x, y).
- Verify solutions in both original equations.

## Watch out for these traps
- Forgetting the y-coordinate after finding x.
- Substituting the wrong equation (use the simpler one).
- Misinterpreting tangency — it means Δ = 0, not "close to the curve."

## Model responses

Use these to check your thinking after you have had a go.

- *y = x² and y = x + 6.* (3, 9) and (−2, 4).
- *x² + y² = 10, y = x + 2.* (1, 3) and (−3, −1).
- *y = x + 5 vs y = x².* Δ = 21 > 0: secant.
- *Sum 15, product 56.* 7 and 8.
- *y = x² + 4 and y = 2x.* Δ = −12 < 0: no intersection.
- *Tangent condition.* k = −1, tangent at (1, 1).
