VC2 Curriculum Compactor

Research basis (Reis & Renzulli). Curriculum compacting eliminates content a student has already mastered to free time for enrichment or acceleration. The NRC/GT study found 40–50% of material could be compacted for targeted students, and compacted students in science scored significantly higher on post-tests than non-compacted peers.
Pre-assessment, not certification. The compactor view identifies CDs where diagnostic performance suggests mastery. Teacher or parent review is essential — a strong selected-response score does not guarantee deep understanding. Use the override toggle to include or exclude CDs from the compacting plan.
